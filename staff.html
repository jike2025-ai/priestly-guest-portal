Front desk guide

Quick reference for the team. Colours and steps used across this page.

Colour guide

Checking a guest in

  1. Find the booking in Reservations (search the name, phone or reference).
  2. Confirm the guest's name and the number of guests with their ID.
  3. Give the apartment door passcode from the apartment card (Door code).
  4. Create their Wi-Fi voucher and welcome card with Welcome card & Wi-Fi.
  5. Check-in is any time on arrival day.

Checking a guest out

  • Check-out is by 12:00 noon on the departure day. The apartment shows orange on that day.
  • Inspect the apartment, then release the caution fee as per house policy.
  • The apartment returns to green (Available) once the stay ends.

Walk-in guests

  • Use New walk-in booking. Dates are blocked on the website immediately.
  • Record how they paid (cash, transfer, POS) or choose Not yet paid.
  • The calendar shows walk-ins with a teal WALK-IN mark.
  • A walk-in can be cancelled from its details panel. Online bookings cannot be cancelled here.

Food and drink orders

  • Guests who are lodging order from the website menu. The order arrives on the front desk WhatsApp.
  • Confirm stock, reply with the total, then deliver to the apartment door.
Open front desk WhatsAppView menu & prices

Online bookings

  • Guests pay online. Confirmed means payment was received.
  • Awaiting payment (purple, striped) is a short hold while the guest is paying. It is released automatically if they do not finish.
  • Two bookings can never overlap on the same apartment. The system blocks it.

Your login

  • Sign in with your own PIN only. Never share it. Every sign-in, booking and room update is recorded under your name.
  • Always press Sign out at the end of your shift.
  • You can cancel a walk-in only if you saved it yourself in the last hour. For anything else, call the admin.

Cleaning and maintenance

  • On Apartments, set each apartment to Clean, Needs cleaning or Maintenance.
  • After a guest checks out, mark it Needs cleaning. When housekeeping is done, mark it Clean.
  • For repairs, choose Maintenance and write what is wrong.
