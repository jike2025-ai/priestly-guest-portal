Alexa setup

Owner page. Connects each apartment's Echo and its arrival scene. Needs your secret key (the same one as in Vercel, ALEXA_KEY).

Secret key

Kept only for this browser tab.

Step 1: show each Echo to the system

  1. Stand at the apartment's Echo and say: "Alexa, open Priestly Welcome".
  2. Come back here and press Refresh. The Echo appears at the top as NEW.
  3. Choose its apartment in the list and it saves by itself.

Step 2: choose each apartment's arrival scene

Create one Tap-to-Run scene per apartment in the Smart Life app (curtains, lights, AC), then pick it here. It runs once, on the guest's first welcome.
