Pick your apartment and dates, pay securely online, and your receipt arrives by email. Rates are per night: arrive any time on your check-in day, and every night ends with check-out by 12:00 noon the next day.

Guest registration details are required for every stay. Your NIN and address are kept securely, seen only by Priestly Apartments management, and never shared on your receipt.

Requests are not part of your online payment. Our front desk will contact you to confirm them and any extra cost, such as airport pickup.
