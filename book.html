Book your stay

Pick your apartment and dates, pay securely online, and your receipt arrives by email. Rates are per night: arrive any time on your check-in day, and every night ends with check-out by 12:00 noon the next day.

You will be taken to Paystack to pay by card, bank transfer or USSD. Your dates are held for 15 minutes while you pay. The caution fee is part of your total and is refundable after check-out, once our team has inspected the apartment.
